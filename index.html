Built by a supplier

I've spent fifteen years making wedding cakes in County Fermanagh, working alongside the photographers, florists and venues who make these days beautiful.

In that time I've watched couples exhaust themselves chasing suppliers, and watched good suppliers lose money on platforms that sent them nothing. Amorelle is my attempt at something fairer for both.

Suppliers apply to join and everyone is welcome — long-established studios and businesses taking their very first booking alike. I started with no portfolio and no orders, just a stall at a wedding fair, so I'm not about to stand at the door deciding who's good enough. Couples do that, with their own eyes.

What we'll never do is sell position. Nobody pays to appear ahead of anyone else, because your wedding shouldn't be decided by who spent the most on advertising.
